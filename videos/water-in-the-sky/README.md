# What if all of Earth's water teleported into the sky?

A 46 s vertical (1080x1920, 30 fps) "what if" short in the cartoon-3D style: toon-shaded three.js
scenes with ink outlines, flat 2D cartoon cards (characters drawn with the repo's `toon.ts`),
narration, music and sound effects. Everything is generated by code in this folder.

## Pipeline
| Step | Command | Output |
|---|---|---|
| Earth maps | `node maps/tojson.mjs && python maps/maps.py` | `web/earthmap.png` (land mask + approximate seafloor depth) |
| Voice | `python voice/make_voice.py <piper> <en_US-joe-medium.onnx> 0.81` | `voice/voice.wav`, `voice/timings.json` |
| Timings → web | (in README history) `web/timings.js` = `voice/timings.json` | every shot and reaction is keyed to words |
| Frames | `python -m http.server 8766` here, then `node render.cjs "" frames` | `frames/f0000.png …`, `frames/shots.json` |
| Sound | `python audio/sound.py frames/shots.json voice/voice.wav out/audio` | `mix.wav` + stems; refreshes `audio/signature/*.wav` |
| Encode | `ffmpeg -framerate 30 -i frames/f%04d.png -i out/audio/mix.wav -map 0:v:0 -map 1:a:0 -c:v libx264 -crf 16 -pix_fmt yuv420p -c:a aac -b:a 256k -shortest final.mp4` | final video |

Voice: Piper `en_US-joe-medium` (voice license CC0), installed from PyPI (`piper-tts`, `joe-us-piper-voice`).

## Script (≈163 words, ≈3.6 words/s)
See `voice/lines.json`. Formula from `references/STYLE_BIBLE.md`: paradox hook → setting → escalation →
"But wait…?" → "You see," + everyday analogy → payoff → "Luckily / Unluckily" ironic ending.

Facts used (own calculations from standard figures):
- Ocean volume ≈ 1.335 billion km³ over Earth's 510 million km² ≈ 2.6 km deep layer.
- That layer weighs ≈ 2,620 t/m² vs ≈ 10.3 t/m² for the whole atmosphere → ≈ 254×.
- The atmosphere holds ≈ 25 mm (one inch) of precipitable water on average.
- Sunlight is effectively gone below ~1,000 m of water (the "midnight zone").
- Tallest wave ever recorded: 524 m run-up, Lituya Bay, Alaska, 1958.
- Falling 10 km: free-fall time √(2h/g) ≈ 45 s; heating g·h/c ≈ 9.81·10,000/4,186 ≈ 23 °C.

## Channel signature sounds (`audio/signature/`)
Reuse these in every video:
- `sting.wav`: water-drop plink + three rising marimba notes (D5, A5, D6), under the first word.
- `swoosh_pop.wav`: on every change of place.
- `ding_wah.wav`: bell ding + two-note muted-tuba "wah-wah" under the last line.
